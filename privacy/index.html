Privacy Policy

Resonance · rell.tech · Last updated: October 11, 2026

Resonance ("the app", "we") is a desktop music player: one native app for listening to SoundCloud, Spotify and YouTube Music, with a single library, search and playback across services instead of switching between music tabs. The marketing site for the app is served at https://rell.tech.

The app is operated by Maxim Resonance, based in Bratislava, Slovakia. Contact: maximshool12@gmail.com. This policy explains what data Resonance accesses when you connect your Google Account for the YouTube Music integration, why each piece is needed, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

This policy covers the Resonance desktop app and this website. The website itself never asks for Google sign-in and never receives Google user data; only the desktop app does, and only after you explicitly sign in with Google and grant permission on Google's consent screen.

1. What we access

The app accesses Google user data only after you sign in with Google and grant permission on Google's consent screen. We request exactly the following scopes, and nothing else:

ScopeDataWhy we need it
openid, email, profileGoogle account ID, name, email address and profile picture, read from Google's userinfo endpointTo sign you in, identify which Google account is connected, and show your account name and avatar in the app.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonlyRead-only YouTube data: your liked videos, your own playlists and their contents, search results, video details (title, channel, duration, view and like counts, category), and public channel profiles you open (name, avatar, banner, subscriber and video counts, uploads)To build your YouTube Music library in the app: the Liked section, your playlists, playlist tracks, search, track details and artist/channel pages. Playback itself needs no scope: audio URLs are resolved locally on your device through the bundled yt-dlp sidecar, not through the API.

We deliberately do not request write scopes. Concretely, force-ssl (which rating/like writes would need) is not requested, so the app cannot modify your YouTube likes or subscriptions. We do not read your subscriptions list at all.

2. YouTube API data in detail

When you use the YouTube Music integration, the app calls the YouTube Data API v3 with your access token for the following read-only operations, each tied to a visible feature:

  • videos.list with myRating=like: your liked videos, shown as your Liked tracks. The music-only filter (category 10) is applied locally on your device.
  • playlists.list with mine=true: your own playlists, shown in your library.
  • playlistItems.list: the tracks inside a playlist you open.
  • search.list: results for a search query you type.
  • videos.list by video ID: details (title, channel, duration, views, likes) for tracks already listed.
  • channels.list: public channel/artist pages you open (name, avatar, banner, subscriber and video counts, uploads playlist).
  • Google userinfo endpoint: your account ID, name, email and picture, shown as your connected YouTube Music profile.

By using the YouTube Music integration you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • We use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features of Resonance that you request, as described in sections 1 and 2 above.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell it.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • We do not allow humans to read your Google user data, except (a) with your explicit consent for specific items, (b) when necessary for security purposes such as investigating abuse, (c) to comply with applicable law, or (d) when the data is aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

Resonance's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent or share your Google user data with third parties, and we operate no server that receives it. Requests go directly from the app on your device to Google's servers (YouTube Data API v3, the OAuth2 token endpoint and the userinfo endpoint).

The static website rell.tech is hosted on Vercel. Vercel only serves the static marketing pages and receives no Google user data from the app. We use no cloud database, no analytics SDK and no advertising provider: there is no Supabase, Neon, Postgres, Firebase or similar backend holding your Google data.

We may disclose data if required by applicable law.

6. Storage, retention and security

  • Google user data is stored only on your own device, in the app's local data directory (a folder named BetterSoundCloud inside your operating system's user config directory), in a file called youtube.json. It holds your YouTube access token, refresh token, expiry time, granted scope, and your Google profile fields (account ID, email, name, picture).
  • All communication with Google uses HTTPS/TLS. The token file is written with operating-system file permissions 0600 (readable and writable only by your user account). Tokens are not encrypted at rest beyond these file permissions, and we do not claim otherwise.
  • OAuth client ID and secret values you type yourself are kept in the app webview's localStorage so later token refreshes can reuse them; one-click bundled keys baked into release builds are held in memory only and are never written to any store. Tokens themselves are never kept in localStorage.
  • We store this data for as long as your YouTube Music account stays connected in the app. There is no other copy: no server copy, no backup, no analytics copy.
  • Access is limited to the app running on your device and to the app's sole operator (Maxim Resonance) for maintenance of the published software; no support staff, advertiser or analytics provider can access it.

7. Deleting your data and revoking access

  • In the app: disconnecting YouTube Music (Connected accounts / Settings sign-out) immediately deletes the stored session: the app clears its in-memory state and deletes the local youtube.json token file. Uninstalling the app and removing its BetterSoundCloud data directory removes the data from the device as well.
  • Google-side revocation: you can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revocation the stored refresh token can no longer be used.
  • Email request: to ask us to confirm deletion or for help removing data, email maximshool12@gmail.com from your account address. We respond and complete deletion of any data we hold within 30 days of the request. Because storage is local-only, deletion means confirming the local file is removed and the grant is revoked; there is no server copy to purge.

8. Other data

The Resonance desktop app includes no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting that sends Google user data anywhere. The static website collects no Google user data; it queries the public GitHub releases API only to display the latest download version. We operate no server-side request logs containing your Google data because we operate no server that receives it.

9. Children

The app is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has connected a Google Account, contact us at the email below and we will help remove the local data.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU/EEA under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because your Google user data is stored on your device, you can directly inspect it in the app (connected profile, library) and delete it via in-app disconnect at any time. To exercise any of these rights with our assistance, contact maximshool12@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.
